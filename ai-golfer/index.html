Meet Quinn: an AI golfer's first five videos

Cliff's idea, built out: an AI golf creator with her own Instagram account, who hits trick shots with Kenetik in her bag, switching between cans and shots, made to bring more women to the brand. Who she is, how she looks and sounds come first, then her first five videos. This is a draft for feedback.
What Kenetik is
Kenetik is brain fuel. 12 grams of ketones in a caffeine-free can, or 10 grams in a 2oz shot that lives in the bag. Clear focus for the afternoon round, with nothing keeping you up tonight.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

The look

Who she is. Quinn (a working name), mid-thirties, a very good amateur who plays most afternoons after work. Confident, dry and a little competitive. She lets the shot do the talking. She's built to bring more women to Kenetik, so she's a golfer women would want to play with, not a model holding a club.

How she looks. Natural and sun-tanned, minimal makeup, hair in a low ponytail through a plain cap. Athletic, not glam. Calm intensity: focused over the ball, a small smile after, never mugging for the camera.

What she wears. Clean, unbranded golf apparel in warm neutrals: a cream or white quarter-zip or polo, a navy or sage skort or trousers, white shoes, a plain cap or visor. Blueberry blue (#269dd2) shows up once, on her towel or a headcover. No other brand's logo anywhere: clothes, clubs, bag or ball.

How she sounds. A woman in her thirties, low and warm, dry and unhurried, like a friend on the range. A few words, said under her breath or right after the shot. Never an announcer, never bubbly.

Where she plays. A real-looking public course and range at golden hour: warm light, long shadows, her bag on its stand, a range bucket. Filmed like a friend shooting vertical on a phone, with natural sound: the click, the ball landing, the can cracking.

Her own Instagram account, labeled as AI. Quinn posts from her own account, which Kenetik sets up and runs, so she builds a following of her own. Her bio says she's an AI golfer made by Kenetik and links to drinkkenetik.com. Every post carries Instagram's AI label, which it requires on realistic AI video, and every caption tags @BeKenetik, with her best posts going out as collabs with @BeKenetik. The craft should still survive a slow rewatch: real ball flight, real bounce, real sound.

Every version

Do

  • Make every shot one a skilled golfer could really pull off. Real physics is the payoff.
  • Shoot it vertical, like a friend filming on a phone: handheld, natural sound, nothing polished, with the on-screen text burned in.
  • Switch formats on purpose: the can for the round, the 2oz shot for the turn or the range.
  • Use real photography of our cans and shots, composited in. Never render the product with AI.
  • Keep her hands at mid-distance on the grip, and cut before any close-up of fingers. Hands are where AI shows.
  • Post from her own Instagram account: label every post as AI, keep “AI golfer made by Kenetik” in her bio, and tag @BeKenetik in every caption.

Don't

  • Hide that she's AI, or have her say she's a real person or a real customer.
  • Imply the drink made the shot. Kenetik is part of her routine, never the reason the ball went in.
  • Say she plays better, longer or with more stamina because of Kenetik.
  • Call it an energy drink or a supplement. It's brain fuel.
  • Make her a cartoon: no glow, no slow-mo sparkle, no impossible shots, no big reactions.
  • Show another brand's logo on her clothes, clubs, bag or ball.
  • Use a real golfer's face, name or signature trick shot.
  • Grade it cool or blue, or add neon.

The bottom line

A golfer women want to play with, who happens to be AI and always has Kenetik in the bag. Real-looking shots, labeled as AI, and the can is her routine, never the reason the ball went in.
