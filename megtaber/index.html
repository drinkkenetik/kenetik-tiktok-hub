Your brief, Megan

UGC tips for creators, from a working actor and singer in NYC with a 15 month old.
The moment

First thing in the morning, before a remote workday and a toddler. You need to be on, and caffeine makes you shaky and on edge.

Who you're talking to

Aspiring UGC creators who come to you for how to start working with brands, plus busy working moms who juggle a career and a little one.

Your angle

Your talking-to-camera videos convert best, so lead there. The story is calm: you felt on edge and snappy on too much caffeine, and your mornings feel calmer and clearer now. Keep it to your own experience and let your honesty sell it.

Working moms see themselves in the first second, and the specific age makes it real.

“My calm morning, as an actor, singer and mom in NYC.”

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say brain fuel once.
  • Lead with talking to camera, your highest converting format.
  • Tell the calmer-morning story as your own experience.
  • Show yourself drinking it.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the working mom and UGC pro who stays calm and clear all morning on brain fuel. Talk to camera, tell your own story honestly, and let it sell itself.
