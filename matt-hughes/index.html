The goal

We are testing hooks. A hook is the first thing you say or show in a video. We want to find the ones that make people stop and watch. Try these at the very start of each video.

What Kenetik is

Kenetik is brain fuel. It helps you focus and think clearly, with no caffeine and no crash. It comes in cans and shots. Always start with a real problem or feeling, then show Kenetik as the fix.

Pick a tab

Getting past price

Every so often, use a hook from the Getting Past Price tab. It helps new viewers see why Kenetik is worth it.

New flavor drops and restocks

If Matt is part of a new flavor drop or a restock of a sold-out flavor, there are tabs for those too.

Keep it simple

Say the hook in the first few seconds, plain and natural. Then show Kenetik. One idea per video. Keep it easy.

The Kenetik way

We will put brain fuel on the map by working smarter, not harder.

The bottom line

Pick the tab that fits Matt, open with the problem, show Kenetik as the fix, and say brain fuel once. Keep it real and simple. The videos that land, we run as paid ads, and that is how everyone earns more.
