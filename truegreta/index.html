Your brief, Greta

Skincare, beauty, wellness, fitness and fashion for women over 40.
The moment

First thing in the morning instead of coffee, and again around 2pm. The honest version is that you and your partner go through them faster than you can keep the fridge stocked.

Who you're talking to

Women over 40 who follow you for what actually works. They read labels, they have tried the jittery stuff, and they are past being sold to.

Your angle

You switched from regular energy drinks that left you jittery and anxious. Your words were less brain fog, more mental clarity, no crash. That contrast is the whole story and it is yours to tell. Talking head is your strongest format, so lead there.

What a great video covers
1Hook

Open in the first 1 to 3 seconds with one of your lines below.

2The problem

A full day of work and content at 40-plus, where the old answer was an energy drink that made you jittery and anxious before it wore off.

3Kenetik

Crack one open, show your honest take. Say "brain fuel" once. Caffeine-free is the twist.

4Close

One reason to try it (caffeine-free, 12g of ketones) and your code TRUE or your link.

Talking head first, voiceover and day-in-the-life as the change-up. Your getting-ready and desk setups both work. Now that you have an assistant repurposing to other platforms, shoot vertical and keep the first three seconds clean so the clip travels.

Your 5 hooks
1
“I am 40-plus and I finally stopped reaching for an energy drink in the morning.”
Video ideaTalking head at your vanity or kitchen counter, coffee cup pushed aside, Kenetik opened.
Why it worksYour audience is exactly this person. Naming the age bracket in the first second tells them it is for them.
2
“Jittery and anxious was the trade I used to make. Not anymore.”
Video ideaStraight to camera, calm delivery, can in hand for the back half.
Why it worksNaming the feeling exactly is what holds people. Keep it to your own experience rather than a promise for anyone else.
3
“The 2pm version of me used to be useless.”
Video ideaQuick cut from a flat afternoon at your desk to you back at it with a can.
Why it worksThe afternoon slump is the single most recognised moment we have, and you live it twice a day.
4
“My partner keeps stealing these out of the fridge.”
Video ideaOpen the fridge, count what is left, mock outrage, then drink one anyway.
Why it worksIt is funny, it is true, and a product two people fight over reads better than any review.
5
“No caffeine. Twelve grams of ketones. That is the whole pitch.”
Video ideaHold the can, read the front panel, let the label do the talking.
Why it worksYour audience reads labels. Giving them the facts plainly respects that and separates us from the shelf.
Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say brain fuel once.
  • Open with caffeine-free in the first three to five seconds.
  • Lead with talking head, your strongest format.
  • Tell the jittery-to-clear story as your own experience.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the woman over 40 who stopped trading jitters for focus. Talk straight to camera, say caffeine-free early, and let brain fuel carry the rest.
