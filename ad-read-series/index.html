Your 3-read series with Kenetik

Three reads, one story: what Kenetik is and when you reach for it, Kenetik inside your content, then the ask. Tell each one your way.
What Kenetik is
Brain fuel. A caffeine-free drink with 12 grams of ketones, for the afternoon wall, the long edit and the late session, with nothing keeping you up after.
The 3 reads, in order

Run them in this order, a week or two apart. Each read builds on the one before, so the ask lands on an audience that already knows Kenetik from you.

1Week 1
Meet Kenetik
The jobIntroduce it. Most of your audience has never heard of Kenetik, so this read is just what it is and when you reach for it. No hard sell.
What it could sound like (not a script)“This is Kenetik. It's brain fuel, 12 grams of ketones and no caffeine, and it's what I reach for when the afternoon hits and I've still got work to do.”
What to showThe can in your hand while you say it, then a real shot of you drinking it in that moment: at the desk, in the car, before the edit. One honest moment from your day beats a product close-up.
The close“If you're curious, my code's in the description.”
2Week 2 or 3
Show it
The jobWork it into the content. This one shouldn't feel like an ad break: Kenetik shows up inside something your audience already watches you do. Show more than you tell.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Kenetik break, then we're locked back in.”
What to showBuild a bit around it: a challenge, a bet, a race against the clock, a taste test with your crew or your family. Keep the can in the scene, let people react on camera, and keep the talking to one line.
The close“Code's in the description if you want to try it.”
3Week 4 to 6
Now's the time
The jobAsk for the order. They've seen you drink it a few times now, so this read gives them a reason to try it this week, with your boosted code and a clear end date.
What it could sound like (not a script)“You've seen me drinking Kenetik for a few weeks now. If you've been curious about brain fuel with no caffeine, this is the week to try it.”
What to showCan in hand, straight to the lens, early in the video. Put your code on screen while you say it and pin it in the comments.
The close“Use my code for the bigger discount. It ends this Sunday, so now's the time to grab it.”
Every read

Do

  • Put each read in the first third of the video, not ten minutes in.
  • Call it brain fuel when you say what it is.
  • Keep the reads in order, a week or two apart.
  • Use the same code and link in all three reads.
  • Mark every read as a paid partnership, and tag @BeKenetik on Instagram clips.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Open with the code. The first two reads earn the third.
  • Read it off a page. Your words beat ours.
  • Skip ahead to the ask, or stack two reads in one week.

The bottom line

Introduce it, show it, then ask. By the third read your audience already knows Kenetik from you, so the code is the easy part.
