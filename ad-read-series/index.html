Your first 3 Kenetik shout-outs

Introduce it, show it, then ask. Three shout-outs, one story: what Kenetik is and when you reach for it, Kenetik inside your content, then the ask. Tell each one in your own voice.
What Kenetik is
Brain fuel. A caffeine-free drink with 12 grams of ketones, for the afternoon wall, the long edit and the late session, with nothing keeping you up after.
The 3 shout-outs, in order

Run them in this order, a week or two apart. Each shout-out builds on the one before, so the ask lands on an audience that already knows Kenetik from you.

1Week 1
Meet Kenetik
The jobStart with the problem, and make it yours. Most of your audience has never heard of Kenetik, but they all know the afternoon wall. Tell them when it hits you, what you used to reach for, and why you reach for Kenetik now. No hard sell.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Every day around 2pm my brain just checks out, and another coffee means I'm wide awake at midnight. So now I reach for this. It's Kenetik, brain fuel with 12 grams of ketones and no caffeine.”
What to showShow the wall first: the half-finished edit, the slump at your desk, the coffee you're about to pour. Then the can, and a real shot of you drinking it in that moment. Skip the product close-up: one honest moment from your day is the shot.
The close“If you're curious, my code's in the description.”
2Week 2 or 3
Show it in action
The jobSeeing is believing. This one doesn't explain Kenetik, it shows it working in your day: the wall hits mid-task, you crack a can, and we watch you get back to it. Let the footage do the convincing.
What it could sound like (not a script)“2pm wall, Kenetik break, and we're locked back in.”
What to showFilm it as it happens: you stalling halfway through the edit, the set or homework with the kids, then the can, then you back at it and finishing what you started. Put the time on screen, 2:05pm can open, 5:30pm still at it, and let the people around you react on camera.
The close“Code's in the description if you want to try it.”
3Week 4 to 6
Now's the time
The jobAsk for the order. They've seen you drink it a few times now, so this shout-out gives them a reason to try it this week, with your boosted code and a clear end date.
What it could sound like (not a script)“You've seen me drinking Kenetik for a few weeks now. If you've been curious about brain fuel with no caffeine, this is the week to try it.”
What to showCan in hand, straight to the lens, early in the video. Put your code on screen while you say it and pin it in the comments.
The close“Use my code for the bigger discount. It ends this Sunday, so now's the time to grab it.”
Every shout-out

Do

  • Put each shout-out in the first third of the video, not ten minutes in.
  • Call it brain fuel when you say what it is.
  • Keep the shout-outs in order, a week or two apart.
  • Use the same code and link in all three shout-outs.
  • Tick YouTube's paid promotion box on every video, and tag @BeKenetik on Instagram clips.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Open with the code. The first two shout-outs earn the third.
  • Read it off a page. Your words beat ours.
  • Skip ahead to the ask, or stack two shout-outs in one week.

The bottom line

Introduce it, show it, then ask. By the third shout-out your audience already knows Kenetik from you, so the code is the easy part.
