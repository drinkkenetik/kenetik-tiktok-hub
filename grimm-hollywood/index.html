Your brief, Grimm

Marathon gaming in character: Grimm Hollywood, the 3000-year-old immortal turned the most famous actor in the world, with next-level production, real skill and humor.
The moment

The dip deep in a marathon stream. You told us coffee crashes you out fast when you are gaming and entertaining 13 to 14 hours a day, and that is your moment: the hour when most streamers fade, and you crack a can and stay in character for chat.

Who you're talking to

Gamers who come to you for the production, the gameplay and the laughs, and who stick around for the long stints because something wild is always about to happen.

Your angle

Two stories, one character. On stream, Kenetik is how an immortal keeps the show going through a living-on-stream run. In short form, it is part of a new chapter: a gamer taking on training and bodybuilding with a Goggins mindset. Keep it about your head and your drive: locked in for the long session, motivated to finally get active. Never a performance promise.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say caffeine-free first, and brain fuel once.
  • Stay in character. Grimm can sell it better than any ad read.
  • Keep it in your head and your drive: locked in, motivated, still sharp late. Never stronger, faster or bigger.
  • Tell the training story as yours, step by step. Only say what is true for you.
  • Put your code and link in every stream description and every clip.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.
