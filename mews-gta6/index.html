Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Post all three around the launch: one before November 19, one on launch night, one that week.
  • Show it in real moments: the countdown, hour four of the session, the morning after.
  • Say caffeine-free first, then brain fuel and ketones if it comes up.
  • Keep it in your head: locked in, calm, clear. Never faster reactions or better aim.
  • Use your own gameplay, never Rockstar's trailer or logo, and tag @BeKenetik on Instagram.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.
