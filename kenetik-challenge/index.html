The 21-Day Kenetik Challenge

For 21 days, have a Kenetik every afternoon so you can feel the afternoon focus for yourself, consistently, over time, not just from one can. Skip it on days 19 and 20, then share what you honestly noticed. Along the way you'll make two kinds of content: updates in TRYBE while you're doing it, and one testimonial video at the end, which we'll run as an ad. We'll send you plenty of Kenetik for all 21 days. No script, just your real experience.
The checklist
How we think about Kenetik
Caffeine-free brain fuel. 12 grams of ketones for clear, steady focus in the afternoon, with nothing keeping you up tonight.
The goal
The whole point is to show people, not tell them, how Kenetik becomes your daily ritual: film it in your real day as it happens, then tell the whole story in one video. Real stories like yours are what we'll run as Meta ads in January, when everyone is starting the new year fresh.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

Questions you'll get
Do I have to say I loved it?
No. We want your honest experience. If something didn't land for you, say so. Real reactions are what make these worth watching.
What if I don't notice anything?
Tell us that too. We'd rather know, and it helps us learn who Kenetik is really for.
Do I have to give up coffee?
No. Keep your coffee if you love it. If you've been meaning to cut back, the challenge is a good excuse to, but that's completely up to you.
Who can join?
Any of our TRYBE creators, at any age or stage. What matters is that you actually do it, and show it.
Will I have enough Kenetik?
Yes, plenty. We'll send enough for all 21 days and then some. If you start running low, tell Nick and we'll send more.
Where do my videos go?
Into TRYBE, like your other Kenetik videos. Your testimonial is made for our Meta ads in January, and your updates may run too. You earn your usual TRYBE commission on sales from your videos.
Do I post them on my own account?
You don't have to. This is content for our ads. If you'd like to share your journey with your followers too, go for it, and disclose the partnership (#ad).
When should I start?
As soon as your Kenetik arrives, ideally by early December, so your 21 days wrap up before the holidays and your story is ready for the new year.
How many should I have a day?
One a day is plenty for the challenge. Have it at the same time each afternoon so you can tell what changes.
Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Actually do it: one every afternoon, with days 19 and 20 off.
  • Fill in the tracker each day, and show it in your updates.
  • Show it, don't just say it: film Kenetik in your real day, as it happens. Short and unpolished beats produced.
  • Say brain fuel once, and that there's no caffeine.
  • Submit every clip in TRYBE, especially the two days off.
  • End your testimonial with an ad close: Tap Shop Now.

Don't

  • Fake it or script it. If you didn't notice something, don't say you did.
  • Make the days off about feeling awful. Keep it to what you missed, like that afternoon focus.
  • Promise results or talk about health conditions. Stick to your afternoons and your focus, in your words.
  • Call it an energy drink or a supplement.
  • Read out a code or a website in your testimonial. The ad's Shop Now button does that.
