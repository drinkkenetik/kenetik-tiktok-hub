The 21-Day Kenetik Challenge

Not a script, and not an ad read. Drink Kenetik every day for 21 days, take two days off, and tell us honestly what you noticed. Film bits of it as you go. Your real experience is the whole point.
How we think about Kenetik
Caffeine-free brain fuel. 12 grams of ketones for clear, steady focus in the afternoon, with nothing keeping you up tonight.
The goal
We want you to actually live with Kenetik for three weeks and share the journey: day 1, the weeks in between, day 21, and the two days after you stop. Honest stories like yours are what we'll run as ads in January, when everyone is starting the new year fresh.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

The 3 angles
1
Days 1 to 21: drink it every day.
WhyPick one moment and stick with it: the afternoon slump, a long stretch of deep work, a late night of gaming. Then check in on camera on day 1 and about once a week. Short and real beats polished. Where were you, and how did the afternoon go?
What it could sound like (not a script)“I'm having a Kenetik every day for the next 21 days, right when I'd usually reach for another coffee. Let's see if I notice a difference.”
2
Day 21: what you noticed.
WhySay what actually changed for you, in your own words: your afternoons, your focus, skipping that second coffee. If you didn't notice much, tell us that too. We'd rather hear the truth, and your audience can tell the difference.
What it could sound like (not a script)“That's 21 days of Kenetik. Here's what I noticed, honestly.”
3
Days 22 and 23: take two days off.
WhyThis is the part people remember. You often don't know how much something is working for you until you stop. Tell it straight, whatever you find, and keep it about your afternoons and your focus.
What it could sound like (not a script)“I took two days off Kenetik to see if I'd actually notice. Here's how it went.”
How to close

When you post, send people to drinkkenetik.com with your code. Then send Nick your clips, the raw check-ins too, so we can turn the best of them into January's ads.

See it in action: one full read (an example, not a script)

“Three weeks ago I swapped my afternoon coffee for Kenetik. Then I stopped for two days to see if I'd notice.

If you don't know it, Kenetik is brain fuel: 12 grams of ketones and no caffeine.

For 21 days I had one every afternoon, right when I'd usually grab another coffee. [Your honest take on the three weeks.]

Then I took two days off. [What you actually noticed.]

If you want to try it yourself, my code is in the caption.”

Questions you'll get
Do I have to say I loved it?
No. We want your honest experience. If something didn't land for you, say so. Real reactions are what make these worth watching.
What if I don't notice anything?
Tell us that too. We'd rather know, and it helps us learn who Kenetik is really for.
Will you use my videos in ads?
We'd love to, with your OK. The best stories will run as ads in January. We'll check with you before any of yours run.
When should I start?
As soon as your cans arrive, ideally by early December, so your 21 days wrap up before the holidays and your story is ready for the new year.
Do I have to post every check-in?
No. Post what fits your feed. A Stories series as you go works great. Send us everything either way, even the clips you don't post.
How many should I have a day?
One a day is plenty for the challenge. Have it at the same time each day so you can tell what changes.
What if I run low on cans?
Tell Nick, and we'll send more so you can finish all 21 days.
Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Actually do it: every day for 21 days, then two days off.
  • Drink it at the same moment each day, so you can tell what changes.
  • Film as you go. Short, unpolished check-ins beat one big produced video.
  • Say brain fuel once, and that there's no caffeine.
  • Disclose the partnership (#ad) whenever you post.
  • Send us every clip, even the ones you don't post.

Don't

  • Fake it or script it. If you didn't notice something, don't say you did.
  • Make the days off about feeling awful. Keep it to what you missed, like that afternoon focus.
  • Promise results or talk about health conditions. Stick to your afternoons and your focus, in your words.
  • Call it an energy drink or a supplement.
  • Skip the two days off. That's the part people remember.
