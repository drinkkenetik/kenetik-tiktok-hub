Your 5 hooks
01
“Up at 3am for the gym. This is the only thing that makes me feel actually awake, not just caffeinated.”
Video idea: Talk to camera in the dark pre-gym, then crack a Kenetik and head out.
The hour is the credential. Nobody argues with 3am, so the next sentence is earned before you say it. And “awake, not caffeinated” names a difference your crowd has felt without having words for, which holds attention better than any benefit would. Film it genuinely early or it shows.
02
“Energy drinks used to wreck me by 2pm. Now I train early and stay sharp all day with this.”
Video idea: Quick before/after: the crash vs the steady day. Fast cuts.
Two versions of the same day give the viewer something to run against his own afternoon, and it costs him nothing to keep watching. Keep the contrast about how the day went — the training, the 2pm hour — not about how you look or what changed in you.
03
“No crash. That is the whole thing. Brain boost in the morning, still going at 2pm.”
Video idea: Straight to camera, one line, show the can, back to it.
The strength here is the flatness: three words, no pitch, said like someone with nothing to sell. Be aware that crash-led opens are our weakest theme at 7% conversion, so this only earns its place if the 2pm half arrives immediately. Make still going at 2pm the real hook.
04
“If you are grinding before the sun is up, you need clean fuel, not another energy drink. Here is mine.”
Video idea: Gym b-roll with a talk-over, Kenetik as the swap.
An identity call-out in the first second lets the viewer sort himself in or out, and the ones who stay are the only ones who were ever buying. BrainMD built its engine on exactly that move. Use your own session for the footage, because borrowed gym b-roll kills the credibility the line depends on.
05
“I stopped chasing caffeine and started fueling my brain. Caffeine-free, no crash, all day.”
Video idea: Honest to-camera, your real switch, the can in hand.
“Chasing caffeine” describes something your viewer is doing right now and has not said out loud — the second scoop, the third can before lunch. Naming the behavior stops him harder than the swap does. Say what your own number used to be; the specific is what makes it his story too.
Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say "brain fuel" once.
  • Open on the 3am grind or the 2pm dip in the first few seconds.
  • Lean into the no-crash angle, it is your edge.
  • Talk to camera, your strength, and mix in gym b-roll.
  • Show yourself actually drinking it.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the 3am grinder who ditched energy drinks for clean brain fuel with no crash. Hook fast, name the crash, show the swap, say brain fuel once. Keep it real. The videos that land, we run as paid ads, and that is how you keep earning.
