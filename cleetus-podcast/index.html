3 angles for Cleetus

No script. Talk about how you actually use it, in your own words. The examples below only show the idea.
How we think about Kenetik
Caffeine-free brain fuel for afternoon clarity and focus, without messing with your sleep.
The goal
When your listeners feel that 2 or 3pm pull toward a coffee or an energy drink, we want them to think of Kenetik instead.
The 3 angles
1
The 2pm swap: your afternoon can
WhyEveryone hits the wall around 2 or 3pm and grabs a coffee or an energy drink to get through it. That reach is the moment we want Kenetik to own. If it is part of your afternoon, say when you drink it and what you used to grab instead. That is what makes a listener swap theirs.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Around two o'clock I used to grab a coffee. Now I grab one of these, and I've still got the whole shop day ahead of me.”
2
Clear for the rest of the day, and it doesn't wreck your night
WhyAn afternoon coffee keeps working long after you need it, and people feel that at bedtime. Kenetik has no caffeine, so you get a clear head for the afternoon and nothing in it keeping you up. Calm and locked in, not wired.
What it could sound like (not a script)“If I have a coffee at three, I'm staring at the ceiling at midnight. This doesn't do that to me.”
3
What it is: caffeine-free, made with ketones
WhyLead with caffeine-free. That is the part that surprises people and makes them ask. Ketones are the answer when they do: brain fuel, the same thing your body makes on its own.
What it could sound like (not a script)“There's no caffeine in it. It's ketones, which is fuel for your brain.”
How to close

Send them to your code: CLEETUS at drinkkenetik.com.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Talk about how and when you really drink it.
  • Mention caffeine-free first, then ketones.
  • Keep it short. One real moment beats a list of features.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Read it off a page. Tell it from how you actually use it.

The bottom line

Next time you would reach for an afternoon coffee or energy drink, grab a Kenetik. If listeners remember one thing, that is it: caffeine-free afternoon focus, without messing with your sleep. How you say it is up to you.
