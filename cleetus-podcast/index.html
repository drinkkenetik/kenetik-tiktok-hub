Your 3 talking points, Cleetus

Three things to work into the podcast when Kenetik comes up. Say them your way. This is what we want every listener to walk away knowing.
The 3 things to say
1
It's not an energy drink. Zero caffeine. It's brain fuel.
Say it like“This isn't an energy drink. There's no caffeine in it at all. It's ketones, brain fuel, and it still gets me locked in.”
WhyYour listeners have tried every energy drink on the shelf. "No caffeine, and it still works" is the one thing that makes them stop and ask what it is.
2
It's what I drink late. In the shop at 9pm, before a late race, on a long night.
Say it like“When I'm in the shop at nine at night, I don't want a coffee or an energy drink. I crack a Kenetik, stay locked in, and I can still go to bed after.”
WhyThis is our focus right now: the late-day moment. Everyone knows the 4pm energy drink they pay for at midnight. Kenetik is the one you can drink late with nothing in it keeping you up.
3
No jitters, no crash. Calm and locked in, not wired.
Say it like“No jitters, no crash later. I'm not bouncing off the walls, I'm just dialed in.”
WhyIt's the difference people feel on the first can. Wired versus locked in is easy to picture and easy to repeat.
How to close

Send them to your code: CLEETUS at drinkkenetik.com. One line is enough, for example: "Use my code CLEETUS, try one late at night and tell me I'm wrong."

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say brain fuel at least once.
  • Tell it as your own story: when and where you actually drink it.
  • Name a real late moment: the shop, race night, a long drive home.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's brain fuel, and that contrast is the point.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Read it word for word. Say it the way you'd say it.

The bottom line

No caffeine. Drink it late. Nothing keeping you up. If listeners remember one thing, it's that Kenetik is the drink for the late shift that doesn't wreck your night.
