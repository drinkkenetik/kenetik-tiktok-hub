Nightlife: the drink in your hand

For creators who go out: concerts, dinners, game nights, late shifts, a night with friends. More people are skipping drinks on a night out, and nobody wants a 10pm energy drink that keeps them up until 3. Kenetik is the drink for the night you want to enjoy and still sleep after.
The swap
Skip the late-night energy drink. Kenetik is caffeine-free brain fuel, so you stay sharp tonight and still sleep when you get home.
The cue
Not drinking tonight? Hold a Kenetik. Something good in your hand, and no explaining required.
The moment

Late in the night, when the group is still going and you want to keep up. That's when people grab an energy drink, then end up staring at the ceiling at 3am.

Who you're talking to

Adults who go out but want to wake up feeling like themselves: people drinking less, the designated driver, the friend with an early workout, and anyone done with 10pm energy drinks.

Your angle

Open on the night: the venue, the table, the group. Then the swap. Everyone else is grabbing an energy drink, you've got a Kenetik, and you're still sharp tonight and still sleep when you get home.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Show the night: the venue, the table, the friends. Keep the can in frame.
  • Say caffeine-free and brain fuel once each.
  • Keep Kenetik as its own drink. Don't mix it into anything on camera.
  • Talk about sleep the way you feel it: you're out late and still sleep fine. No health or recovery claims.
  • Film two or three versions of your opener. We test hooks against each other in ads.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

The night is the moment. Open on where you are, make the swap, and land the reason: caffeine-free brain fuel, so you're sharp tonight and still sleep after.
