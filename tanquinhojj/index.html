Your brief, Tanquinho

Jiu-jitsu world champion, academy owner, coach and family man. A full day on the mats and off them.
The moment

The middle of the day, when your focus starts to drop. Classes, privates, running Soul Fighters and time with your family all stack up, and that is when most people reach for another coffee. You reach for Kenetik instead: before you step on the mats, between coaching sessions, or when you need your head back for a busy afternoon.

Who you're talking to

Jiu-jitsu practitioners, martial artists, coaches and people who train hard and live busy lives. They follow you for technique, discipline and how you actually run your day, and they are as selective about what they put in their bodies as you are.

Your angle

You are the real thing: a world champion and former UFC fighter who still teaches every day. You said you do not want it to feel like a traditional ad, and that is the right instinct. Show how you actually use it through your day, and say why caffeine-free matters to you in your own words.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say caffeine-free first, and brain fuel once.
  • Show it in a real moment: before the mats, between classes, at the front desk.
  • Keep it to your head: focus, clarity, being present for your students and your family. Never stronger, faster or better cardio.
  • Talk to camera the way you already do. No script, and only what is true for you.
  • Tag @BeKenetik on Instagram.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the world champion who still runs a full day on and off the mats, with his head clear for all of it on caffeine-free brain fuel. Show the day you are already having, and let the can live in it.
