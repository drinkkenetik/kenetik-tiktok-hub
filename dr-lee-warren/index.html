Talking points for Dr. Warren

For your podcast mid-rolls, your YouTube integrations and the Short. There’s no script. Tell it in your own words, from how you actually use it. The example read below only shows the idea.
How we think about Kenetik
Kenetik is metabolic brain fuel. A caffeine-free drink with 12 grams of ketones, a fuel your brain can use alongside glucose. Clear, steady focus, without messing with your sleep.
The goal
Your listeners trust you on the brain. We want them to learn from you that the brain has a second fuel, and to think of Kenetik the next time they reach for another coffee.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

The 3 angles
1
The neurosurgeon’s take: your brain has a second fuel
WhyThis is the line only you can say. The brain runs mostly on glucose, and it can also use ketones for fuel: when ketones rise in the blood, the brain takes them up and burns them. Kenetik is 12 grams of ketones in a can. Say it the way you’d explain it to a friend, in one or two plain sentences. We’re happy to send you the studies.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Most people don’t know this, but your brain can run on more than sugar. It can use ketones for fuel too, and Kenetik is a drink with 12 grams of them.”
2
Off the caffeine treadmill
WhyCoffee to get going, another to keep going, and each cup buys a little less than the last. By the afternoon your listeners are tired and wired, and the last cup is still working at midnight. Kenetik is caffeine-free, so it’s the swap for that afternoon cup: clear focus for the rest of the day, with nothing in it keeping them up tonight.
What it could sound like (not a script)“I used to run on coffee all day and pay for it at night. Now my afternoon drink is Kenetik. No caffeine, so nothing’s keeping me up that night.”
3
Clear for what matters most
WhyYour listeners care about showing up well, for their families, their work and their calling. The afternoon slump costs them that, and so does the coffee that keeps them up. Kenetik’s moment is the hour when there’s still more to give. Say what that hour looks like for you.
What it could sound like (not a script)“The hours after lunch used to be my worst. Now that’s when I get my writing done, and there’s still something left for my family at night.”
How to close

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Tell it as yourself: what you drink, when, and what you notice. Your listeners trust your experience more than our words.
  • Give the science in one plain line: the brain can use ketones for fuel, not just glucose.
  • Say brain fuel and caffeine-free at least once.
  • Say that Kenetik sponsors the episode, in your own words.
  • Say your code out loud, with the code and link in the show notes or description.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink or a supplement. It’s caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Give medical advice, or say you recommend it to patients. You’re sharing what you drink.
  • Read it like an ad. Your words beat ours.
