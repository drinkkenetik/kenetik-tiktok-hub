Start Here — the brief

Read this first, then pick your audience above
The goal

We are testing hooks. The first 1 to 3 seconds of a video decide whether someone keeps watching, so we want to find the opening lines that make people stop scrolling for Kenetik. Your job is simple: try these hooks at the very start of your videos and see what lands.

What Kenetik is

Kenetik is metabolic brain fuel: clean, caffeine-free focus. The winning angle is always the same. Lead with the person’s problem, or the moment they feel it, then bring in Kenetik as the fix. Never open with caffeine or ketones. Open with the feeling.

How this page works

There are three audience tabs above: Focus & Productivity, Calm Clear Focus, and Endurance & Competition. Pick the one that best fits the people who follow you. Each tab has a short set of hooks to test, with a quick video idea under every one.

How to use the hooks

Open your video with the hook in the first 1 to 3 seconds. Say it straight to camera, or put it as bold text on screen, then go into your Kenetik moment. Test a few different hooks and lean into the ones that get the most watch time and comments.

What makes a hook work

Hook first, no warmup. Keep it native and real, like a post, not an ad. Name a specific moment your audience actually feels. And let Kenetik arrive as the answer, not the opening line.

Ready to go

Pick your audience tab above and start testing. Post what feels natural to you, tag us, and we will amplify what performs.

Kenetik is clean, caffeine-free brain fuel, so always lead with the person’s problem, then the fix.
