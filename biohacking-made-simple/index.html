The goal

Make biohacking feel simple. A lot of people are curious about it but put off: it sounds expensive, weird and complicated. Your job is to translate it into one easy habit, the way you would explain it to a friend over coffee.

Who you're talking to

People who are curious about biohacking but allergic to hype. Often 40-plus, smart and busy. They trust someone who keeps it plain and admits what they don't know.

What Kenetik is

Kenetik is brain fuel: 12 grams of ketones, no caffeine, nothing artificial. It comes in cans and shots.

Pick a tab

Biohacking Made Simple has the hooks that make it feel easy. Real Proof has the hooks that borrow authority we actually have: Dr. Nick Norwitz, Dave Asprey and the Informed Sport certification. The Norwitz Experiment leads with his "hack a 7-day fast in 24 hours" story.

Format

Talking head works best, with the can in frame early. A kitchen counter or desk beats a studio. Keep it under 60 seconds and let one idea carry the video.

The bottom line

Make biohacking feel easy. One habit, one plain fact, real proof and your honest take. Say brain fuel once and let the simplicity sell it.

The Norwitz experiment

6 hooks  ·  HACK A 7-DAY FAST IN 24 HOURS
The story

Dr. Nick Norwitz (Harvard-trained doctor, Oxford PhD in ketone research) wrote about a study where 12 people fasted for 7 days and their ketones climbed to about 4.0 by day seven. He then ran his own test: a 24-hour fast with two Kenetik shots near the end, and reached 4.4, higher than the week-long fasters, in one day. He picked Kenetik because it is bioidentical D-BHB. Open with his headline, credit him by name, then give your own take.

His ketones hit 4.4. Higher than a week of fasting, in one day. He picked Kenetik because it’s bioidentical ketones. No caffeine, nothing artificial. I’m not fasting for a week, but this is the brain fuel I actually reach for. [One line on how it fits your day.] Use my code [YOUR CODE] if you want to try it.”
