Your GTA 6 voiceover

Take a clip from your own GTA 6 gameplay and do a voiceover on it about Kenetik. The idea is up to you: go full comedy like Bob Menery, who calls everyday chaos like the last seconds of the Super Bowl (watch a few on his Instagram), or start from one of the ideas below.
The checklist
What you get
A 24-pack of Kenetik in any flavor you like, or a variety pack, plus shots and merch once your video is live.
For our favorites
Extra cash for a few months of GTA+.
The moment

Hour five of a GTA 6 session. Chat is still going, the heist still isn't done, and you're still locked in.

Who you're talking to

Your chat: gamers grinding GTA 6 late into the night, who already know the energy drink routine.

Your angle

Make Kenetik the star of the clip, not a can at the end. Funny, straight or somewhere in between: whatever fits your channel.

What a great video covers
1Hook

Open in the first 1 to 3 seconds with one of your lines below.

2The problem

Long sessions run late, and the usual midnight can is still working at 4am, long after you log off.

3The solution

Kenetik is the turning point of your clip. Say brain fuel once, and that there's no caffeine.

4Close

End on the moment, not a sell. Tag @BeKenetik in the caption.

Vertical (9:16) and short. Your own gameplay only: never Rockstar's trailer or logo, and never anyone else's clips. Going the Bob Menery route? Skip the impression and his audio.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Use your own gameplay and your own voice.
  • Make Kenetik part of the story, not a can tacked on at the end.
  • Keep it in your head: locked in, calm, clear. Never faster reactions or better aim.
  • Follow the gamer one pager for how to talk about Kenetik.
  • Add #ad to your caption, or say you're a Kenetik partner.
  • Send it to Lawrence first, and post once you get the OK.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.
