What Kenetik is
Kenetik is brain fuel. Caffeine-free, made with 12 grams of ketones. Clear, steady focus for the afternoon, without messing with your sleep. Three new flavors to show: Raspberry Lychee, Golden Pear and Dave Asprey's Blueberry Acai.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

The problem we solve
It's 2pm and the day isn't done: a class to coach, a session to run, a drive home. Most people reach for another coffee or an energy drink, it gets them through the afternoon, and then it's still working at midnight.

Every spot

Do

  • Shoot vertical (9:16) first for TikTok and Reels. Get the to-camera piece horizontal too.
  • Let Matt say it in his own words. The lines above are starting points, not a script.
  • Say brain fuel and caffeine-free at least once, with the can's label facing the camera.
  • Film the first sip every time and hold on the reaction, plus B-roll of all three flavors: in hand, in the fridge, the crack and the pour.
  • Get a signed release from everyone on camera, and Grey Team's OK before filming their space, so we can run the videos as ads.
  • When Matt posts, add the TikTok Shop product link and send us a Spark code for each video so we can boost the ones that land.

Don't

  • Talk about anyone's health, injuries, treatment or medical history, Matt's and the Grey Team crew's included.
  • Call it an energy drink, a supplement or a pre-workout, or tie it to training, recovery or performance results.
  • Name a price or a discount on camera. We add the offer in the edit, so the clips keep working after Black Friday.
  • Show or name another drink brand, or pair Kenetik with alcohol.
