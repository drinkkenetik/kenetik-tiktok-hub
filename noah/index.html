Two a month, pick any of these

The moments we can clip.

These are the organic ones. No code, no link, no sell, and nothing here needs staging. If one happens while the camera is rolling, let it run a few seconds longer and we will find it.

One thing separates a clip we can use from set dressing: one honest sentence, said out loud, that stands up on its own. A can on the desk is a prop. A can in your hand while you say what you actually think is a clip.

Two a month, thirty to sixty seconds

The two you actually script.

These go inside a long video, anywhere in the first third. Code NOAH out loud, link in the description. How you deliver it is entirely yours. Both angles below are already proven, so treat them as the spine and not the script.

1
The switch, already made
Report a decision you already took, in the past tense. What you cut down on, and the brain fuel that replaced it. Both halves out loud, because the version that only names what you quit leaves people with nothing to move to. This is our strongest hook family and the only one winning on TikTok Shop and in our paid ads at the same time.
3.9x · strongest thing we run
2
The hour your brain goes quiet
Film it when it is actually happening. Open on the distinction: not tired, just flat. Then say why. Your brain is underfuelled rather than short of a stimulant, and more of a stimulant does not fuel anything. Hold it for a second before you bring the can in.
Holds 42% of everyone who starts it, the highest we have

Close both by saying shop now out loud, not just on screen. It is the line that gets dropped most often and the one that costs the most when it goes.

The two that do not work

“Energy drinks won’t do it. Stop drinking them.”

Our worst opening, by a distance. Strange, because the best one is also about energy drinks. The difference is who is talking. Repeating what people already say lands. Telling someone to quit the thing in their hand does not.

And never open on the discount. Deal-led content is the worst performing thing we make. The code belongs at the end of an integration and nowhere at all in an organic one.

The only three asks

So we can actually cut it.

Say “brain fuel” once. Not the whole spiel, just those two words somewhere. It is the category we are building, and it is what lets a clip stand on its own when we pull it out of a twenty minute video.
Keep the can in frame for a beat. Not held up like a commercial. Visible. On the desk, in your hand, in the cup holder.
Let the moment run. The two seconds after a real reaction are usually the part we want. Do not cut it tight.
The guardrails, and there are only two

What we will never ask you to say.

That it fixes, treats or cures anything
That it is an energy drink, a pre-workout or a supplement

Saying what caffeine did to you is completely fine and it is usually the best part of the video. Wired, jittery, could not sleep, all of it. Just never put Kenetik on the other side of it as the cure. Beyond that: caffeine-free, sugar-free, twelve grams of ketones, clarity and focus. That is the whole approved list and everything else is your own experience.

You are early to this on purpose.

Your dad got here first and proved the thing sells. What has not happened yet is somebody making brain fuel make sense to people who are still figuring it out. Four moments a month and two you script, and that is the category taking a shape it does not have yet.
