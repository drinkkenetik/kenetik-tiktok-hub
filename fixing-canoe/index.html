Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Keep the can on the desk, in frame for the whole stream, and in hand on swap meet runs.
  • Say code CANOE on stream, pin drinkkenetik.com/CANOE in chat, and add both to your video descriptions.
  • On swap meet videos, a quick tag works: "5 AM, no coffee, no crash. This is my brain fuel. They just got into QuikTrip, so if you've got one near you, check it out. Code CANOE in the description."
  • Keep QuikTrip to "just got into QuikTrip." It's in Tulsa-area QTs right now.
  • Mention you're a Kenetik partner, or add #ad to descriptions.
  • Tag @BeKenetik where you can.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's brain fuel.
  • Say "zero sugar, zero caffeine." "No caffeine, no crash" is good to go.
  • Say it's safer than other drinks, or that it fixes or treats anything.
  • Say it's in every QT.
  • Read it off a page. Your words land better than ours.

The bottom line

Hours into the stream and still locked in. Say what it is, how you found it and what it lets you do, then close on QuikTrip and code CANOE.
