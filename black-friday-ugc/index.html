Black Friday UGC: lead with the deal

For videos that will run in our Black Friday ads. Kenetik is a premium brain fuel and we rarely go this deep on price, so this is the one time we open on the sale, then get to the problem it solves.
The deal
Up to 44% off when you subscribe. Code FOCUS30 takes 30% off on top of our 20% subscription discount, through December 1. A deal this size on a premium brain fuel doesn't come around often.
The reason to buy
Caffeine-free focus for the afternoon, without wrecking your sleep.
The deadline
The sale goes live in our paid ads on Monday, November 9. Try to get your video into TRYBE before then so it can run from day one, but we'll still take submissions after.
The moment

The afternoon, when the day isn't done but your focus is. That's when people reach for another coffee, and that's the moment to show.

Who you're talking to

People scrolling for a Black Friday deal who haven't tried Kenetik yet, or who've been waiting for the right price. The deal stops their scroll, and the problem is what gets them to buy.

Your angle

Open on the deal and when it ends. Then make it personal: the afternoon slump, and how you get through it without another coffee.

What a great video covers
1Hook

Lead with the deal in the first 1 to 3 seconds, on screen and out loud, with one of your lines below.

2The problem

Every afternoon you hit a wall. Another coffee gets you through it, but it's still working at bedtime, so tomorrow starts tired.

3The solution

Name the fix first: focus that gets you through the afternoon without costing you sleep. Then show that Kenetik is how you get it. Crack one open, give your honest take, and say “brain fuel” once. Caffeine-free is the twist.

4Close

Say the deal one more time, then: “Click the link below to get the deal before your favorite flavor sells out again.”

FOCUS30 works on drinkkenetik.com, so keep it out of your TikTok Shop posts, where TikTok runs its own Black Friday deals.

Say it rightKenetik is said “kuh-NET-ik,” like kinetic. Ketones is said “KEE-tones.”

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Put up to 44% off when you subscribe on screen in the first second, and say it out loud.
  • Say subscribe. That's what takes the deal all the way to 44%.
  • Show the can, and say brain fuel once.
  • Film two or three versions of your opener. We test them against each other in our ads.
  • Try to get your video into TRYBE before Monday, November 9 so it can run from day one. We'll still take it after.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.
