The hook

Nine seconds decided it.

“If these sell out anywhere as quick as these did, then you might not even get these before you see this video.”

That is the whole opener. No product name, no ingredients, no pitch. Later in the video he lands the other half: metabolic brain fuel, caffeine-free.

Why it worked

Three things, and you can copy all three.

1
It gives you a reason to move now

Not “this is good.” “You might miss it.” That is the difference between a review and a reason. Reviews get watched. Reasons get bought.

2
It is true

Golden Pear genuinely sold out in four weeks. He is not manufacturing pressure, he is reporting something that happened. Viewers can hear the difference and the comments will tell you when they do.

3
He still says what it is

Scarcity gets the attention. Brain fuel tells them what they are actually buying. Skip that half and you have hyped a mystery, which is why the openers that never name the category stall out.

Your turn

How to run this in your own voice.

Do not copy his words. Copy the shape. The words have to be yours or it reads as a read.

1
Open on the risk of missing it

First three seconds, your own phrasing, before you say anything about ketones or caffeine. Those come later.

2
Get the can on camera

In your hand, not on a pedestal. Crack it if it is sealed. The sound is part of it.

3
Say brain fuel out loud, once

Every video. It is the category we are building and it only works if everybody says it.

4
One honest line about why you drink it

Not a list of features. One sentence that would still make sense if somebody clipped it out on its own.

5
Say shop now out loud

Out loud, not just on screen. This is the step that gets dropped most often and it costs the most when it goes.

The one rule

Never invent a sellout.

Golden Pear is the only flavor that has ever sold out, so it is the only one you can say it about. Raspberry Lychee is limited edition, which is true and is plenty. A shortage nobody can verify gets called out in the comments, and that costs you more than the video earns.

Before you post

Two things we will never ask you to say.

That it fixes, treats or cures anything
That it is an energy drink, a pre-workout or a supplement

Saying what caffeine did to you is fine and it is usually the best part of the video. Wired, jittery, could not sleep, all of it. Just never put Kenetik on the other side of it as the cure. Beyond that: caffeine-free, sugar-free, 12 grams of ketones, clarity and focus.
