The goal

We are testing hooks for your streams and clips. The first few seconds decide whether a viewer stays, so drop these at the top of a clip or work them into a stream moment. We will watch what lands and lean into the winners.

What Kenetik is

Kenetik is metabolic brain fuel: clean, caffeine-free focus. It is the edge that keeps your mind sharp when it matters. Lead with the win or the moment, then bring in Kenetik as the fuel behind it.

Your audience

Your viewers do not all come for the same thing. Some want to game at a higher level, some want to clean up their wellness, and some want to get more done in their day. We built a tab for each, all in your voice, so you can speak to whoever is watching.

The tabs

Gaming Performance, Wellness, and Productivity each target a different part of your audience. Raspberry Lychee has hooks for the new flavor launch. Pick the tab that fits the moment or the crowd.

One rule

No medical claims. Kenetik does not cure, treat, or fix anything. Keep it to focus, feel, and winning. That is also TikTok Shop’s rule.

Let’s go

Clear mind. Clean fuel. Dominate. That is the Kenetik way.
