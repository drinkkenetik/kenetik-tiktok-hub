Stream Talking Points

No script. Work it into the stream the way it really fits, in your own words. The examples below only show the idea.
How we think about Kenetik
Caffeine-free brain fuel for afternoon clarity and focus, without messing with your sleep.
The goal
When your chat reaches for an energy drink before a long session, we want them to think of Kenetik instead: locked in for the late game, with nothing in it keeping them up after they log off.
The 3 angles
1
The all-nighter, minus the 4am wired
WhyGamers play long, and most of them fuel it with energy drinks. The caffeine is still working when they log off, so they lie there wired and start tomorrow wrecked. Kenetik is caffeine-free brain fuel: calm, clear focus for the long session, and nothing in it keeping you up after. That late session is the moment we want Kenetik to own with gamers. Show it mid-stream, and say what you used to grab instead.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Hour five of the stream and my head's still in it. The old move was an energy drink at midnight, then staring at the ceiling at 4am. This is brain fuel with zero caffeine, so when I log off, I'm actually done for the night.”
2
Calm in the clutch, not wired
WhyEveryone in your chat knows the energy drink shakes, right when the round matters, and the crash two hours later. Kenetik has no caffeine, so the focus comes on steady: calm and locked in instead of wired and scattered. Talk about how your head feels, never that it makes anyone aim better or react faster.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Energy drinks used to give me the shakes right when the round mattered. Now it's this. Calm, locked in, head in the game, and no jitters.”
3
Nothing else like it
WhyGamers know every energy drink on the shelf, so say what Kenetik is not first, then what it is: caffeine-free brain fuel, 12 grams of ketones. Being the one thing in its lane is the reason to try it.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Chat, this is not an energy drink. There's no caffeine in it. It's brain fuel, 12 grams of ketones, and honestly there's nothing else like it for a long night of gaming.”
How to close

Send them to your code: DOC at drinkkenetik.com.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Show it in a real moment of the stream: hour five, the late ranked game, the clutch round.
  • Say caffeine-free first, then brain fuel and ketones if it comes up.
  • Talk about how your head feels: locked in, calm, clear.
  • Keep it short. One real moment beats a list of features.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Read it off a page. Tell it from how you actually use it.

The bottom line

Next time chat would crack an energy drink for a long session, grab a Kenetik. If they remember one thing, that is it: locked in for the late game, with nothing in it keeping you up after. How you say it is up to you.
