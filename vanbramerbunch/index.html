Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say brain fuel once.
  • Lead with the afternoon moment.
  • Talk to camera like you're telling a friend about a find.
  • Show yourself drinking it.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the mom who keeps up with the kids all afternoon on clean brain fuel, no jitters. Start with the 3pm wall, share it like any find you love, and keep it real.
