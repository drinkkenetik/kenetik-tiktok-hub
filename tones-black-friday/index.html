Every version

Do

  • Make the Raspberry Lychee can the hero, with real raspberries and lychees, like your Golden Pear video.
  • Model the sauna on the real Hot Box: thehotboxsauna.com
  • Burn the on-screen text in, big and readable. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • Use the brand colors, plum to sky blue: #7B2A86, #5A3E9E, #3557B7, #2A9AD3.
  • Say entries for the sweepstakes. Points are only for Insiders rewards.
  • Deliver 9:16 first. We'll ask for 1:1 and 16:9 once it's approved.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink, or show another brand's can.
  • Make a health claim, or show it fixing sleep.
  • Make a coffee drinker the joke. Poke fun at the cup, not the person.
  • Put a dollar value on the prize.
  • Show Golden Pear (sold out) or the new Asprey flavor (not out yet).
  • Show sugar-free or zero-sugar badges.

The bottom line

The 2pm wall, the can, then the sauna. Show why Kenetik is the better afternoon drink, then give people a reason to order this month: every order enters them to win.
