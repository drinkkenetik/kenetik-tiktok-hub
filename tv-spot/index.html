Making a Kenetik TV spot

Everything you need on one page: what Kenetik is, the problem it solves, real creator videos that sold, and four ways to turn that into an ad. The goal of the spot is a habit: when viewers feel the afternoon pull toward a coffee or an energy drink, they think of Kenetik.
What Kenetik is
Kenetik is brain fuel. Caffeine-free, made with 12g of ketones. Clear, calm focus for the afternoon, without messing with your sleep.
The problem we solve
It’s 2pm and you hit the wall. You reach for a coffee or an energy drink, it gets you through the afternoon, and then it is still working at midnight. Tomorrow starts tired, and you reach for more.

Turn the problem into an ad
1
The 2pm reach
The setupTwo people at the same desk at 2:30pm, both hitting the wall. One reaches for a coffee or an unbranded energy drink. The other reaches for a Kenetik. Both get the afternoon done.
The turnSame night, split screen. The caffeine side is in bed at 12:40am, eyes wide open, staring at the ceiling. The Kenetik side closes the laptop at 10:30, flips the light off, and the room goes dark.
The line“Hit the 2pm wall? Reach for Kenetik. Nothing keeping you up tonight.”
2
The caffeine treadmill
The setupA loop that keeps speeding up: coffee at 7am, energy drink at 3pm, wide awake at midnight, more coffee at 7am. Same face, more tired every lap.
The turnAt 3pm the hand reaches for the energy drink and grabs a Kenetik instead. The loop stops. The music settles.
The line“Get off the treadmill. Brain fuel, zero caffeine.”
3
Calm, not wired
The setupA shop at 3pm. With the energy drink, everything is loud: buzzing sound, shaky camera, fast cuts, a dropped socket.
The turnCut to the same scene after a Kenetik: the noise drops out, the shot holds still, the wrench turns clean. Same afternoon, a different head.
The line“Calm. Clear. Locked in. Brain fuel, not caffeine.”
4
What time is it for you?
The setupQuick portraits of real afternoon walls, each with the time on screen: a nurse at 2pm mid-shift, a parent at 3pm before pickup, a mechanic at 2:30.
The turnEach one cracks a can and gets back to it, steady. End on the can and the time.
The line“Whatever your 2pm looks like, Kenetik is brain fuel for it.”
Every spot

Do

  • Say brain fuel out loud, and show the can with 12g of ketones and zero caffeine.
  • Put a real time on screen: 2pm, 2:30, 3pm. The afternoon wall is the story.
  • Play the Kenetik character calm and steady, never hyped.
  • Keep the energy drink side relatable and funny, not mean.

Don't

  • Blur the line between the two cans. Kenetik is brain fuel, and the contrast is the point.
  • Show or name a real energy drink brand. Use plain, unbranded cans.
  • Say or show that Kenetik helps you sleep or sleep better. The point is that nothing in it keeps you up.
  • Claim it treats stress, anxiety or anything else, or make nutrition claims like sugar-free.
  • Air a script, voiceover or on-screen line before Kenetik has checked the claims.

The bottom line

When you hit the 2pm wall, reach for Kenetik. The coffee or energy drink gets you through the afternoon and costs you tonight. Kenetik is caffeine-free brain fuel for the afternoon, without messing with your sleep.
