Your brief, Andrei

Parkour with a story: more than flips, how you push yourself physically and mentally.
The moment

Before a session. You told us you drink it in the morning before the gym, and that is your moment: the minutes before training when you are getting your head right for what you have planned. The same goes for a second session later in the day, when most people would grab a coffee or a pre-workout.

Who you're talking to

People who follow you for movement and self-expression, not just the tricks: young athletes and creatives who want to push themselves and like seeing the work behind it.

Your angle

Cinematic storytelling is your strength, so tell a story, not an ad. The movement is the hook, and the focus behind it is the story. Kenetik is part of how you get your head right before you train. Keep it about your mind: locked in and present for the movement, never jumping higher or recovering faster.

What a great video covers
1Hook

Open in the first 1 to 3 seconds with one of your lines below.

2The problem

Getting your head right before a session, without the jittery edge of a pre-workout or the midday crash after the morning coffee.

3Kenetik

Crack one open, show your honest take. Say "brain fuel" once. Caffeine-free is the twist.

4Close

One reason to try it (caffeine-free, 12g of ketones) and your code ANDREI15 or your link.

Instagram Reels first, TikTok second. Your cinematic edits are the format: a slow build, the spot, the run-up, the move. Put the can in the story the way a filmmaker would, in the prep, at the spot before the line, on the walk home. Keep any line about Kenetik short, in voiceover or on-screen text.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say caffeine-free first, and brain fuel once.
  • Keep it in your head: locked in, clear, present for the movement. Never higher, faster or stronger.
  • Tell it as your own routine. The morning before the gym is perfect.
  • Only say what is true for you.
  • Tag @BeKenetik on Instagram.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the athlete who trains his mind as hard as his body. Tell the story behind the movement, let Kenetik live in the prep, and say brain fuel once.
