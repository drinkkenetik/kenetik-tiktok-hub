Your brief, Ty

Active college life in Charlotte: golf team, run club, and whatever the day turns into.
The moment

Mornings, before a workout or a run, and on the days you go straight from class to golf to running without stopping.

Who you're talking to

College students and young athletes who follow you for an active day done properly rather than a highlight reel.

Your angle

You said it yourself, you want it to feel like part of your lifestyle rather than a standalone ad. So do not make an ad. Film the day you were already having and let the can be in it. Subtle placement is your instinct and it is the right one.

What a great video covers
1Hook

Open in the first 1 to 3 seconds with one of your lines below.

2The problem

A day that runs class to golf to run club with no gap in it, where the usual fix is more caffeine before you have even left the house.

3Kenetik

Crack one open, show your honest take. Say "brain fuel" once. Caffeine-free is the twist.

4Close

One reason to try it (caffeine-free, 12g of ketones) and your code TTIMEDAILY or your link.

Day-in-the-life and active content, TikTok first. Natural integration beats a product moment. Golf course light in the morning and the walk between class and practice both shoot well. Keep it vertical and keep it quick.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Say brain fuel once.
  • Open with caffeine-free in the first three to five seconds.
  • Integrate it into the day rather than stopping for a product moment.
  • Shoot for TikTok first, it is where your audience is.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink (you're contrasting against them).
  • Make any health or medical claim.
  • Open with caffeine or ketones.
  • Over-produce it.
  • Cram everything into the opener.

The bottom line

Your lane is the college athlete whose day never stops and who does it without caffeine. Film the real day, drop brain fuel into it, and keep it as subtle as you said you wanted.
