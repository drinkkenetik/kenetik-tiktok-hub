Your three concepts, ready to film. We kept your structure and changed a few lines to keep them on brand for Kenetik. Every changed part is marked Updated, and the reasons are at the bottom.
What Kenetik is
Caffeine-free brain fuel for afternoon clarity and focus, without messing with your sleep.

What we changed, and why
  • The Kenetik line says what it is. Caffeine-free brain fuel, 12 grams of ketones. A line about what it is designed to do for your focus becomes a claim once it runs as an ad, so it stays out. On camera, say “12 grams”, not “12g”.
  • Sleep comes up only as what Kenetik leaves out. “Nothing in it to keep you up tonight” works. That it helps anyone sleep never does. Concept 3’s line also said “caffeine” twice, so we cut one.
  • Focus, not energy. Concept 2’s second follow-up now asks about focus only. We never describe Kenetik in terms of energy.
  • “Shop now” at the end of every video. After the closing question, the host says “shop now” to camera, so every video has a cut we can run as an ad.
  • A target on every concept. Concepts 1 and 3 make the five everyday videos. Concept 2 makes the five menopause videos, with a woman host talking to women 40 and up.
  • A personal-experience note on concept 2. Kenetik is not a menopause product, so nobody on camera says it fixed their fog, memory, hormones or sleep.
Every video

Do

  • Ask the hook before anyone says the brand name.
  • Let them answer in their own words, wrong guesses included.
  • Film in the afternoon, when people feel the wall.
  • Film the first sip every time.
  • Get a signed release from everyone who makes the final cut, so we can run the videos as ads.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It’s caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Keep a line where someone says it treats, fixes or helps with a symptom or with sleep. Cut it in the edit.
  • Script their answers, or use a friend and pass them off as a stranger.
  • Name a price or a discount on camera.
  • Ask anyone whether they are in menopause.

The bottom line

Ten videos: five everyday, five for women over 40. Ask first, let them talk, say what Kenetik is in one sentence, film the first sip, and close with “shop now”.
