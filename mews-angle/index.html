Talking Points

No script. Show it the way it really fits your day, on stream, in the gym or at the desk, in your own words. The examples below only show the idea.
How we think about Kenetik
Caffeine-free brain fuel for afternoon clarity and focus, without messing with your sleep.
The goal
When your audience reaches for a coffee, a pre-workout or an energy drink to push through, we want them to think of Kenetik instead: locked in for the stream, the session and the work, with nothing in it keeping them up tonight.
The 3 angles
1
Gaming late, locked in
WhyLong sessions run late, and most gamers fuel them with an energy drink that is still working when they log off, so they lie there wired. Kenetik is caffeine-free brain fuel: calm, clear focus for the late game, and nothing in it keeping you up after. Show it mid-stream, and say what you used to grab instead.
What it could sound like (not a script)“It's midnight, I'm three hours into the stream, and my head's still in it. I used to crack an energy drink right about now and be wide awake at 4am. This is brain fuel with zero caffeine, so when I log off, I'm actually done.”
2
The gym and the to-do list
WhySame idea, earlier in the day: the pre-workout or the afternoon coffee to push through training, editing and everything else on the list, and then the stream on top. Keep it in your head, not your body: focus, the mind-muscle connection, getting through the list. Never more reps, bigger lifts or a better workout.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Training, then editing, then streaming tonight. I used to stack a pre-workout and an afternoon coffee just to get through it. Now it's one of these. Clear head, no jitters, and I'm still good for the stream later.”
3
Off the caffeine treadmill
WhyCoffee to wake up, pre-workout to train, an energy drink to stream, then lying awake and needing more coffee in the morning. Your audience lives that loop, so name it the way it looked in your day, say how you got off it and what you drink instead. Talk about the old habit, never lecture anyone about it.
What it could sound like (not a script)“Coffee to wake up, pre-workout for the gym, an energy drink for the stream, then wide awake at 3am and more coffee in the morning. I finally got off that treadmill. Now it's caffeine-free brain fuel, 12 grams of ketones.”

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Show it in real moments: mid-stream, before a lift, at the edit desk.
  • Say caffeine-free first, then brain fuel and ketones if it comes up.
  • Keep it in your head: locked in, calm, clear. Never stronger, faster or more reps.
  • Keep it short. One real moment beats a list of features.

Don't

  • Call it an energy drink. It's caffeine-free brain fuel.
  • Make any health or medical claim, or say it helps you sleep.
  • Read it off a page. Tell it from how you actually use it.

The bottom line

Next time you would reach for a coffee, a pre-workout or an energy drink to push through, grab a Kenetik. If your audience remembers one thing, that is it: locked in for whatever is next, with nothing in it keeping you up tonight. How you show it is up to you.
